Six-Router Project Update — Test Notes
========================================

Topology
- Six routers: R1-R6.
- Added R2-R6 (base delay 12 ms, bandwidth 70 Mbps) and R6-R5 (base delay 8 ms, bandwidth 80 Mbps).
- DQN state size: 28; action size: 6.

Validation performed
- Integration test: PASSED.
- DQN test run: 30/30 successful routes; mean successful delay 37.69 (simulated ms).
- Paired comparison: 100 traffic seeds per method.
  DQN: success 100.0%, mean successful delay 37.54 ms, mean reward 5.84.
  Static Dijkstra: success 100.0%, mean successful delay 37.06 ms, mean reward 5.82.
  Adaptive Dijkstra: success 100.0%, mean successful delay 37.06 ms, mean reward 5.82.

Interpretation
In this run DQN was about 1.29% slower than Dijkstra on mean delay:
(37.54 - 37.06) / 37.06 * 100 ≈ 1.29%.
So the current evidence does not support a claim that DQN is better. This is useful experimental feedback; improve training/reward/state and rerun before making such a claim.
